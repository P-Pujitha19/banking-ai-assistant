# Current Account

## Overview
- The Current Account is meant for businesses, professionals and self-employed customers who make many transactions every day.
- A current account can be opened by proprietors, partnership firms and companies with valid KYC and business proof.

## Minimum Balance of Current Account
- A current account requires an average quarterly balance (AQB) of ₹25,000.
- If the average quarterly balance falls below ₹25,000 a penalty of ₹750 plus 18% GST is charged for the quarter.

## Interest on Current Account
- No interest is paid on balances in a current account.

## Features of Current Account
- Unlimited credit transactions and free cash deposits up to ₹5,00,000 per month.
- Ten free ATM transactions per month at Fictional Bank ATMs.
- Overdraft facility is available against fixed deposits with the current account.
- Free business internet banking with bulk payment and multi-user access.
