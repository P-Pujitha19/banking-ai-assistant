# Account Features and Digital Banking

## Digital Banking
- Mobile banking app: balance, statements, fund transfer, bill payments, card control and loan applications are available 24x7.
- Net banking provides complete account management and is free for all accounts.
- UPI is available on the mobile app with a single registered virtual payment address.

## Account Services
- Nomination: every account can have a nominee, which can be changed online at any time.
- Standing instructions and auto-debit mandates can be set up for EMI, SIP and bill payments.
- Account alerts: SMS and email alerts are sent for each transaction; SMS alerts cost ₹15 per quarter.
- Joint accounts can be operated as either or survivor, or jointly.

## Debit Card Benefits
- The RuPay debit card includes domestic airport lounge access twice per quarter on spends above ₹10,000 in the previous quarter.
- The debit card has an annual fee of ₹250 plus GST.
