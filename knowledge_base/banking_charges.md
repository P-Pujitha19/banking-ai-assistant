# Banking Charges and Fees

## Common Service Charges
- SMS alert charges are ₹15 per quarter plus GST.
- The debit card annual fee is ₹250 per year plus GST.
- A duplicate physical statement costs ₹100 per request plus GST.
- Cheque book: the first 25 leaves per financial year are free, then ₹4 per leaf plus GST.

## ATM Charges
- ATM withdrawals beyond the free monthly limit cost ₹21 per transaction plus GST.
- Savings accounts get five free transactions per month at Fictional Bank ATMs and three at other bank ATMs in metro cities.
- Balance enquiry and mini-statement at Fictional Bank ATMs are free.

## Fund Transfer Charges
- NEFT, RTGS and UPI transfers made online are free of charge.
- IMPS up to ₹1,000 is free.
- IMPS above ₹1,000 and up to ₹1,00,000 costs ₹5 plus GST per transaction.
- IMPS above ₹1,00,000 and up to ₹5,00,000 costs ₹15 plus GST per transaction.

## GST on Charges
- 18% GST is applied on all bank service charges and appears in your statement as a separate GST entry.
- For example the GST on an SMS alert charge of ₹15 is ₹2.70.
