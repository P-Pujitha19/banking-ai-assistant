# Salary Account

## Overview
- The Salary Account is a special savings account opened through an employer tie-up for receiving monthly salary.
- A salary account is a zero minimum balance account as long as a salary credit is received every month.

## Minimum Balance of Salary Account
- The salary account has no minimum balance requirement while a monthly salary credit is received.
- If no salary is credited for three consecutive months, the salary account is converted to a regular savings account with a minimum average monthly balance of ₹5,000.

## Interest on Salary Account
- The salary account earns interest of 3.50% per annum on all balances.

## Features of Salary Account
- Eight free ATM transactions per month at Fictional Bank ATMs and three at other bank ATMs in metro cities.
- Pre-approved personal loan offers for salary account holders, subject to credit appraisal.
- Free personal accident insurance cover of ₹5,00,000 on the salary account debit card.
