# Interest Rates

## Deposit Interest Rates
- Savings account: 3.50% per annum up to ₹10,00,000 and 4.00% per annum above ₹10,00,000.
- Salary account: 3.50% per annum.
- Current account: no interest.
- Recurring deposit: 6.50% per annum for tenures of 6 months to 10 years.

## Fixed Deposit Interest Rates
- Fixed deposit for 7 to 45 days: 3.50% per annum.
- Fixed deposit for 46 to 180 days: 4.75% per annum.
- Fixed deposit for 181 to 364 days: 5.75% per annum.
- Fixed deposit for 1 year to less than 2 years: 6.75% per annum.
- Fixed deposit for 2 years to less than 3 years: 7.00% per annum.
- Fixed deposit for 3 years to 5 years: 6.75% per annum.
- Senior citizens receive an additional 0.50% per annum on fixed deposits.

## Loan Interest Rates
- Personal loan: 10.99% to 16.50% per annum.
- Home loan: 8.50% to 9.75% per annum.
- Car loan: 8.75% to 11.50% per annum.
- Education loan: 8.90% to 12.00% per annum.
- Loan interest is charged on the reducing balance and the final rate depends on the credit score.
