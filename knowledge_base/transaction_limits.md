# Transaction Limits

## Digital Payment Limits
- The UPI limit is ₹1,00,000 per transaction and ₹1,00,000 per day.
- The IMPS limit is ₹5,00,000 per day.
- NEFT has no upper limit and works 24x7; RTGS has a minimum of ₹2,00,000 per transaction.
- Net banking fund transfer to a newly added beneficiary is limited to ₹25,000 in the first 24 hours.

## Card and Cash Limits
- The ATM cash withdrawal limit is ₹40,000 per day on the classic debit card.
- The point-of-sale (POS) and online purchase limit on the debit card is ₹1,00,000 per day.
- Contactless card payments up to ₹5,000 do not need a PIN.
- Cash deposit limit at branch is ₹2,00,000 per day; PAN is mandatory for deposits above ₹50,000.

## Changing Limits
- Customers can raise or lower their card and UPI limits from the mobile banking app under Manage Limits.
