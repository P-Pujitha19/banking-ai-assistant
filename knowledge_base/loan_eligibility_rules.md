# Loan Eligibility Rules

## Common Eligibility Criteria
- The applicant must be an Indian resident with completed KYC and a Fictional Bank account.
- A credit score of 700 or higher is required for personal loans and car loans.
- A credit score of 725 or higher is required for home loans.
- A credit score of 680 or higher is required for education loans.
- Total monthly EMIs including the new loan must not exceed 50% of monthly income (55% for home loans). This is called the FOIR limit.

## Income and Age Requirements
- Personal loan: minimum monthly income ₹25,000 and age 21 to 60 years.
- Home loan: minimum monthly income ₹40,000 and age 21 to 65 years.
- Car loan: minimum monthly income ₹30,000 and age 21 to 65 years.
- Education loan: minimum monthly income ₹20,000 and age 18 to 60 years.

## How Eligibility Is Decided
- The bank checks age, income, credit score, requested tenure, requested amount and affordability against these rules.
- The maximum eligible loan amount depends on monthly income, existing EMIs, the interest rate and the tenure.
- A higher credit score earns a lower interest rate within the product range.
- Eligibility checks are indicative and the final approval is subject to document verification.

## Documents Required
- Identity and address proof (Aadhaar, PAN, passport), the latest three salary slips and six months bank statements.
- Self-employed applicants need the last two years income tax returns and business proof.
