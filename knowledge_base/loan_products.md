# Loan Products

## Personal Loan
- Personal loan interest rate: 10.99% to 16.50% per annum, with an indicative rate of 11.49% for good credit profiles.
- Personal loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000 with tenure from 12 to 72 months.
- Personal loan is an unsecured loan, no collateral is required.
- Processing fee for a personal loan is 1.5% of the loan amount plus GST.
- Prepayment charge on a personal loan is 2% of the outstanding principal within 12 months and nil afterwards.

## Home Loan
- Home loan interest rate: 8.50% to 9.75% per annum, with an indicative rate of 8.65%.
- Home loan amount: ₹5,00,000 to ₹5,00,00,000 with tenure from 5 to 30 years.
- The home loan finances up to 80% of the property value and is secured by the property.
- Processing fee for a home loan is 0.5% of the loan amount plus GST.
- There is no prepayment charge on floating-rate home loans.

## Car Loan
- Car loan interest rate: 8.75% to 11.50% per annum, with an indicative rate of 9.25%.
- Car loan amount: ₹1,00,000 to ₹50,00,000 with tenure from 1 to 7 years.
- The car loan finances up to 90% of the on-road price of a new or pre-owned car.
- Processing fee for a car loan is 1% of the loan amount plus GST.

## Education Loan
- Education loan interest rate: 8.90% to 12.00% per annum, with an indicative rate of 9.75%.
- Education loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹75,00,000 with tenure from 1 to 15 years.
- The education loan covers tuition, hostel, books and living costs for courses in India and abroad.
- A co-applicant such as a parent is required and the co-applicant income is considered.
- Processing fee for an education loan is 0.5% of the loan amount plus GST.
