# Minimum Balance Requirements

## Minimum Balance by Account Type
- Savings account minimum balance: ₹5,000 average monthly balance.
- Salary account minimum balance: zero, provided a salary credit is received every month.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000 average quarterly balance.

## Penalty for Not Maintaining Minimum Balance
- The penalty for not maintaining the minimum balance in a savings account is ₹300 plus 18% GST per month.
- The penalty for not maintaining the minimum balance in a current account is ₹750 plus 18% GST per quarter.
- There is no minimum balance penalty on salary accounts that receive a monthly salary credit.
- The penalty appears in your statement as MIN BAL PENALTY, followed by a separate GST entry.

## How the Average Balance Is Calculated
- The average monthly balance is the sum of the end-of-day balances divided by the number of days in the month.
- Keeping a fixed deposit of ₹50,000 or more with the bank waives the minimum balance penalty on the linked savings account.
- Customers aged 60 and above (senior citizens) enjoy a 50% concession on the minimum balance penalty.
