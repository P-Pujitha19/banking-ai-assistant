# Savings Account

## Overview
- The Fictional Bank Savings Account is a deposit account for individuals who want to save money and earn interest.
- A savings account can be opened by any resident Indian individual aged 18 years or above with completed KYC.
- Minors can have a savings account operated by a guardian until they turn 18.

## Interest on Savings Account
- The savings account earns interest of 3.50% per annum on daily balances up to ₹10,00,000.
- The savings account earns interest of 4.00% per annum on the portion of balance above ₹10,00,000.
- Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited to the savings account every quarter.

## Minimum Balance of Savings Account
- A savings account requires an average monthly balance (AMB) of ₹5,000.
- If the average monthly balance falls below ₹5,000 a penalty of ₹300 plus 18% GST is charged for that month.

## Features of Savings Account
- A free RuPay debit card is issued with the savings account.
- Free online banking, mobile banking, UPI, NEFT, RTGS and IMPS (IMPS up to ₹1,000 is free).
- Five free ATM transactions per month at Fictional Bank ATMs and three free transactions at other bank ATMs in metro cities.
- A free cheque book with 25 leaves is provided per financial year.
- Nomination facility is available on the savings account and can be updated online at any time.
- Passbook and monthly e-statements are available free of charge.
