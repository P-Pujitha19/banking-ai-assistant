# Banking Policies

## Security and Privacy
- The bank never asks for your PIN, password, CVV or OTP over phone, SMS or email.
- Report suspicious transactions within 3 working days to get zero liability for unauthorised electronic transactions.
- Account numbers are masked in all communication and only the last four digits are displayed.

## KYC and Compliance
- KYC documents must be updated every 10 years for low-risk customers, 8 years for medium-risk and 2 years for high-risk customers.
- Accounts without valid KYC may be restricted until the documents are submitted.

## Grievance Redressal
- Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 30 days.
- If a complaint is not resolved, customers can approach the banking ombudsman of the central bank.

## Charges Policy
- All service charges are published in the schedule of charges and changes are notified 30 days in advance.
- Bank charges are shown in the statement as separate lines with a GST entry of 18%.
