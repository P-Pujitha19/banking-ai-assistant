# Frequently Asked Questions

## Accounts
- How do I open a savings account? You can open a savings account online with Aadhaar and PAN through video KYC or visit any branch.
- How do I change my registered mobile number? Visit a branch with ID proof or update it through net banking using an OTP on your existing number.
- What happens if my account stays inactive? An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 12 months is marked inactive and for 24 months dormant.
- How can I download my account statement? Open net banking or the mobile app, choose Statements and select the dates; e-statements are free.

## Cards and Payments
- What should I do if my debit card is lost? Block the card immediately in the mobile app or call the 24x7 helpline, then request a replacement card.
- Why did my UPI payment fail but money got deducted? The amount is auto-reversed to your account within 5 working days if the payment failed.

## Loans
- Can I prepay my loan? Yes, a loan can be prepaid in part or full; see the product prepayment charges.
- How long does loan approval take? A personal loan is typically approved within 2 working days after documents are verified.
- Does applying for a loan affect my credit score? A loan application triggers a credit enquiry which can lower the score by a few points temporarily.
